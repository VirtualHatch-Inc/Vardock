# Code of conduct

English | [日本語](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ja.md) | [中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh.md)

This code applies to everyone who takes part in Vardock's issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other repository spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep discussion on the technical subject and give specific, actionable feedback.
- Accept that maintainers decide what the project includes, and respect a decision once it is made.
- Protect other people's privacy and credentials, and follow [Security](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory remarks.
- Sexual language or imagery directed at a person.
- Publishing another person's private information, credentials, or private conversations.
- Repeated off-topic posts, spam, or deliberate disruption.
- Posting an actionable exploit or other people's secrets in a public space.

## Scope

This code covers activity in this repository and in other places where a person acts on behalf of the project.

## Reporting

Report a comment, issue, or user with GitHub's report-abuse tools on the repository. Report security matters as described in [Security](SECURITY.md), not in a public thread.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict a person's participation.
